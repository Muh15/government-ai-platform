# HAYAH Pilot — Birth Journey

## Goal
Prove that HAYAH can turn one life event into a coherent government journey and produce measurable operational value.

## Scope
One journey: **New Baby / Birth**. Demo data is synthetic. Real deployment requires approved data, permissions, integrations, security, and official rules.

## 12-week plan
- Weeks 1–2: discovery and journey mapping.
- Weeks 3–6: build HAYAH, HAQ, MAWED and Control Tower prototype.
- Weeks 7–9: approved integrations within pilot scope.
- Weeks 10–12: launch, measure, and executive outcome report.

## KPIs
- Time to identify next required service.
- Navigation steps and cross-agency handoffs.
- Journey completion and drop-off rate.
- Appointment distribution / predicted congestion.
- Beneficiary satisfaction.

## Commercial path
Pilot → measured outcome → 5 journeys → enterprise platform → multi-agency life layer.

## Planning price
Illustrative planning band: **SAR 0.5M–1.5M** for a tightly scoped pilot. Final pricing depends on integration, security, hosting, support, and procurement requirements.
