# HAYAH — Business Model

## Positioning
**HAYAH Platform** is sold as a government life-journey intelligence layer, not as a replacement citizen app.

## Revenue streams
1. Platform license / annual subscription.
2. Implementation and integration.
3. New life journeys and rules.
4. Advanced intelligence modules.
5. Support and SLA.
6. Partner / ecosystem revenue where commercially and contractually appropriate.

## Illustrative commercial ladder
| Stage | Offer | Planning range |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Pilot | SAR 0.5M–1.5M |
| 2 | Enterprise expansion | SAR 2M–5M+ |
| 3 | Annual platform | Recurring |
| 4 | Expansion | Journeys + integrations + modules |

These are internal planning ranges, not official government market prices.

## First wedge
Start with **Birth Journey**. Prove value on one journey, then expand to marriage, home, business, education, retirement and other journeys.

## Sales narrative
**Problem:** services are organized by agencies while people experience life events.

**Solution:** HAYAH understands the event, discovers potential entitlements, orchestrates services, recommends timing, and gives the government a journey-level operational view.

**Commercial CTA:** a controlled 12-week pilot with measurable KPIs.

## Long-term model
The Core stays reusable while journey definitions, rules, integrations, analytics and support expand account value over time.
